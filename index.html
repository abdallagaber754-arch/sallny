Social Video Hub

A personal desktop tool for creators

Social Video Hub runs on your own computer. You connect your TikTok account, choose a video file, and send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You then open the TikTok app, add the caption and privacy settings, and publish it yourself. The app never posts on your behalf.

How it works

  • You sign in with TikTok through the official TikTok authorization screen.
  • The app requests only two permissions: user.info.basic (to show your account name) and video.upload (to send a draft to your inbox).
  • Your video goes directly from your computer to TikTok. There is no server operated by us.
