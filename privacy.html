Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Social Video Hub (“the app”) is provided by Abdallah Gaber Mohamed. The app runs on your own computer. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What the app handles

  • TikTok account information: your TikTok open ID and display name, received when you authorize the app.
  • Access and refresh tokens: issued by TikTok so the app can send drafts on your behalf.
  • Video files you choose: held temporarily on your computer while they are sent.
  • Upload history: the time, account and delivery status of each upload.

Where it is stored

Tokens, account names and upload history are stored only on your computer, in a file encrypted with a key derived from your vault password. The password itself is never saved. Video files are deleted after the upload job finishes; any leftover temporary files are removed when the app starts.

How the data is used

Only to connect your account, upload the video you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draft, and check the delivery status with TikTok. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or profiling, and we do not use it to train any model.

Who receives data

Your video and requests go directly from your computer to TikTok’s servers. TikTok’s own Privacy Policy governs how TikTok handles that data. No other third party receives your data from the app.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account in the app. This revokes the app’s access at TikTok and removes the account from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access from your TikTok app settings at any time.
  • Delete everything by removing the app’s data folder from your computer.

Children

The app is not intended for people under the minimum age TikTok requires to hold an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
